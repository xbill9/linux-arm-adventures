## Incident during the audit: DNS emptied by NetworkManager

At 13:24, `nmcli general reload conf` (for the captive-portal config) made NetworkManager rewrite `/etc/resolv.conf` with **no nameservers**. The USB Ethernet is managed by `ifupdown` + `dhcpcd` (`/etc/network/interfaces`), not by NetworkManager (`enxf8e43b0fecaf:unmanaged`), so NM had no DNS to write. Routing worked; name lookups failed (`Temporary failure resolving 'deb.debian.org'`), and the first install attempt fetched nothing.

Fixed for now by restoring `nameserver 192.168.0.1` (from `/var/lib/dhcpcd/enxf8e43b0fecaf.lease`). **Still open:** the two network managers will keep fighting over `/etc/resolv.conf`. The clean fix is to let NetworkManager manage the Ethernet too (remove the `enxf8e43b0fecaf` stanza from `/etc/network/interfaces`), so it owns DNS for both wired and Wi-Fi. That briefly drops the wired link and may change its IP.
