# 2026-10-10: sid upgrade blacks out every kernel

## Summary

A trixie→sid upgrade brought GRUB 2.14. After that, every kernel (7.2.9 and 6.12.x) went black the instant GRUB handed over, with no output even with `ignore_loglevel earlycon=efifb`. The fix was to put the installer stick's shim + GRUB 2.12 on `/boot/efi` and boot from a hand-written menu. The 6.12.107 kernel and initramfs then booted unchanged. Downtime was about 10:06–11:13.

## Root cause

GRUB 2.14 on `/boot/efi` (sda2), the ESP the firmware actually uses. Evidence:
- The kernel `vmlinuz-6.12.107` on disk is **byte-identical** to the installer stick's kernel, which boots.
- The DTB `c630-6.12.dtb` is **byte-identical** to the stick's.
- The 6.12.107 initramfs was untouched by the upgrade (built 2026-10-09 22:43).
- With these same three files, it failed under GRUB 2.14 and booted under GRUB 2.12.

Not proven: *why* 2.14 fails on this firmware. Suspects include its newer Linux EFI handoff (LoadFile2 initrd, EFI DT fixup) and the 2.14 shim. Not investigated.

## What went wrong in the response

- **Assumed which ESP the firmware boots** from an earlier note ("sdd1 boots GRUB under the Windows Boot Manager name") instead of checking. The sync hook, the sdd1 restore and the stick partition-2 edit all targeted loaders that never run. **Fix:** marker per loader, read off the screen.
- **Called an entry "known-good" without ever booting it.**
- **Stated unverified things as fact**, such as that the backup had no GRUB and that the stick reads the edited file. Each one cost a rescue boot.
- **The user's warnings ("weird setup", "check the initramfs") weren't acted on early.** The initramfs turned out not to be the cause this time, but checking it first was cheap. The real lesson is the boot-path assumption.
